## Project name
AI Conversation Director

## Short description
A Meta-powered co-host that listens to human discussions, waits when it should, and asks grounded follow-up questions when useful.

## Problem and approach
Small creator teams often moderate a discussion while participating in it. This project explores an AI co-host that supports the conversation without responding to every utterance. Meta handles transcription and contextual intervention decisions; the operator retains control over when the co-host speaks. Separate speech synthesis voices the selected text.

## Technology and disclosure
